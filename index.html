Amazon SEO

What is Amazon SEO?

Amazon SEO is the process of optimizing a product listing to improve its visibility, relevance and conversion potential inside Amazon search results.

Core Ranking Logic

  • Relevance: how well the product matches the search query.
  • Performance: how well the product converts after being seen.
  • Sales Velocity: how consistently the product generates sales.
  • Customer Experience: reviews, ratings, price, availability and fulfillment.

Keyword Research

Keyword Types

  • Main Keyword: the primary term that describes the product.
  • Secondary Keywords: supporting terms related to features, use cases or audience.
  • Long-tail Keywords: more specific queries with clearer purchase intent.
  • Competitor Keywords: terms competitors rank for or advertise on.
